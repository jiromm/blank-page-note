# Privacy Policy  
**Effective Date:** October 8, 2025  

Thank you for using **Code Snippets & Notes** (the “Extension”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our Chrome Extension.

---

## 1. Overview
This Extension is designed to help you **create, organize, and access code snippets and notes** in Markdown format — quickly and securely.  
We do **not** collect, store, or share any personal data on external servers. All information stays in your browser.

---

## 2. Data We Collect
We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information (PII) or track your browsing activity.

The only data stored by the Extension includes:
- Code snippets, notes, and links you create  
- Their order and organization preferences  

All of this data is stored **locally** in your browser using the **Chrome Storage API**.  
If Chrome Sync is enabled, your data may be synchronized securely across devices via Google’s infrastructure.

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Used
We use your data solely to:
- Display your notes and snippets in the Extension  
- Provide a seamless experience across sessions and devices (if sync is enabled)

We do **not** use your data for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

---

## 4. Permissions Explained

| Permission       | Purpose                                                    |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| `storage`        | Save and sync snippets and notes locally.                  |
| `clipboardWrite` | Copy snippets to clipboard on user action.                 |
| `clipboardRead`  | Optionally import text from clipboard.                     |
| `activeTab`      | Optionally insert snippets into the current tab on command.|

These permissions are used **only** for their stated purposes and never for background tracking or monitoring.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services
We do **not** use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or external APIs.  
All operations are performed locally within your browser.

---

## 6. Data Sharing
We do **not** share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties.

---

## 7. Data Control & Deletion
You are always in control of your data.  

To remove all stored data:
1. Open Chrome’s **Extension Settings**  
2. Find this Extension and click **Remove**  
3. Optionally, clear data from Chrome Sync if enabled (via *Chrome Settings → Sync*)  

Deleting the extension permanently removes all stored notes and snippets.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy
This Extension is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Effective Date.”  
We encourage you to review it occasionally.
